# Nature Portfolio Reporting Summary — draft answers

The official form is the Nature Portfolio Reporting Summary (filled in during submission).
These are draft answers to copy into it; the corresponding author must check each one.
Every statement refers to files in this repository.

## Statistics

- **Sample size (n) per condition:** given in every table caption: 4,200 generated items
  (controlled studies); 210 items, 30 per category, per model and condition (E5, E6, E8a,
  E10); 300 (E8b) and 297 (E9) SituatedQA questions per model and condition.
- **Repeated measurements:** every model answers the same items under every condition;
  contrasts are paired within model.
- **Statistical tests:** no null-hypothesis significance tests. Paired accuracy contrasts
  are reported with 95% percentile bootstrap intervals (3,000 resamples, seed 20260726),
  clustered on template category (synthetic studies) or on the upstream question
  (SituatedQA studies). With seven category clusters the intervals are approximate and
  reported descriptively (Methods).
- **Covariates / multiple comparisons:** none adjusted; category-level differences are
  labelled exploratory.
- **Effect sizes:** differences in accuracy in percentage points with 95% intervals.
- **Bayesian analysis, hierarchical designs, correlation coefficients:** not used.

## Study design (field-specific section)

- **Sample size:** the controlled benchmark has 600 items per category (4,200), fixed by
  design. Model studies use 30 items per category (210), limited by API cost and the free
  daily quota; SituatedQA samples are 300 items (75 per date type) and 300 items
  (uniform), fixed in the pre-registrations before any call.
- **Data exclusions:** none in E5, E6, E7, E8a, E8b and E10. E8: a model withdrawn
  mid-run was excluded and replaced by dated amendment before outcomes were inspected
  (`paper/PREREGISTRATION_E8.md`). E9: three items deterministically rejected by one
  provider were excluded from both models by amendment before analysis; the full-sample
  result is reported as a sensitivity analysis (`paper/PREREGISTRATION_E9.md`). Earlier
  quota-truncated and aborted runs are archived and enter no result.
- **Replication:** E6 and E8a replicate E5 with six further models from six developers;
  results agree in direction and verdict (Results).
- **Randomization:** item samples drawn with fixed seeds, stratified by category or date
  type; not applicable to group allocation.
- **Blinding:** not applicable to automated scoring; annotation packets are blinded and
  item order is shuffled per annotator.

## Pre-registration

E6/E7, E8, E9 and E10 were pre-registered in the repository before any study call
(`paper/PREREGISTRATION_*.md`; git commit history provides the timestamps); amendments are
dated and were made before outcomes were computed.
