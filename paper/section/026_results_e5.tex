\subsection*{A preliminary matched multi-model intervention (E5)}

The controlled results above establish that the seven applicability invariants
are executable and causally separable under oracle and predicted structured
states (E1--E4). They do not by themselves establish that \emph{prompting} a
language model to emit an explicit situation state improves it. As a first,
deliberately bounded test of that E5 claim, we evaluate three Gemini models
(\texttt{gemini-2.5-flash}, \texttt{gemini-2.5-flash-lite} and
\texttt{gemini-3.5-flash}) on a stratified, evidence-complete rendering of
SituationCatch-Bench (five items in each of the seven categories, $n=35$),
holding the evidence block, decoding temperature ($0$), thinking budget ($0$)
and output-token budget fixed, and varying only the prompt condition among a
plain \emph{direct} answer, a \emph{structured} JSON prompt, and an explicit
\emph{situation}-state prompt. The primary quantity is the within-model
situation-on-versus-off contrast $\Delta$: the situation condition minus the
strongest non-situational prompt for the same model, so any difference is
attributable to the explicit-state instruction rather than to model identity,
evidence or budget. Every raw response, token count and latency is archived, and
a completion audit (\texttt{code/audit\_e5.py}) requires one successful response
for every item, condition and category before these numbers may enter the
manuscript.

\input{section/tables/e5_multimodel_table}

The result is a null-to-negative $\Delta$ (Table~\ref{tab:e5-multimodel}):
asking the model for an explicit situation state matches the best baseline on
the two stronger models ($\Delta=0.0$) and \emph{underperforms} a plain
structured prompt on the smallest model ($\Delta=-5.7$). The situation and
structured conditions also cost roughly three times the output tokens and higher
latency than the direct prompt. In other words, a prompt-level ``situation''
field is not, by itself, situation awareness: consistent with the controlled
finding that accuracy collapses once states must be \emph{sensed} from text
rather than supplied ($100.0\%\rightarrow77.9\%$), the bottleneck is situation
sensing and validation, not the presence of a state slot in the prompt. This
supports the paper's central position rather than a plug-in prompting win, and
it is exactly the kind of measurement the framework is meant to make routine.

These numbers are a single-provider (Gemini-family), small-$n$ pilot with three
prompt conditions; they neither confirm nor refute the situation-engineering
hypothesis at scale. A confirmatory E5 requires multiple model families under
matched budgets, a natural-language evaluation set, strong retrieval and agentic
baselines, human agreement and clustered uncertainty (see the reporting
protocol). The frozen manifest, model snapshots and cost accounting are in
\texttt{code/experiment\_manifest.e5.json}, and the run is reproduced by a single
audited command (\texttt{python code/run\_e5.py}).
